Website Planning Document

Site Name

Name: Cedar3 Technology

Optional Domain: cedar3tech.com

Reasoning: I chose this name because it establishes a strong, memorable brand identity. The word "Cedar" implies strength and growth, while "3" adds a modern, tech-forward element. It serves as a digital hub for our technology solutions, services, and community resources.

Site Purpose

The purpose of the Cedar3 Technology website is to showcase our innovative software and IT solutions. The site will provide detailed information about our services, a portfolio of past projects, and a user-friendly contact form for potential clients to request quotes or technical support.

Scenarios

These are questions that a target audience member might ask when visiting the site:

  • What kind of custom software development services does Cedar3 Technology offer for small businesses?
  • How can I contact the Cedar3 support team for urgent IT troubleshooting?
  • Where can I view a portfolio of past projects and read client testimonials?

Color Schema

I have selected a professional and modern "tech-nature" color palette that will be applied to the site plan and the final project:

Primary Color: #1b4332 (Deep Forest Green)
Used for: Main headings, header background, and footer background.
Secondary Color: #74c69d (Mint Green)
Used for: Call-to-action buttons, links, and accents (like the line under the h2 tags).

Typography

I will use two Google Fonts to establish a clear visual hierarchy:

  • Montserrat (Sans-Serif): Used for all headings (h1, h2, h3). It is bold, modern, and grabs attention.
  • Open Sans (Sans-Serif): Used for paragraph text, lists, and navigation. It is highly readable and clean.
